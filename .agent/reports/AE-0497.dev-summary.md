## Developer Completion Report
Ticket: AE-0497

### Summary
The "quality score" tile showed the share of published posts ×100 when no score existed (and "0%" with no
posts). Per the triage recommendation (Q2 option a, listed as start-now), `quality_score_average` is now
the average `overall_score` of the `rubric_evaluation_scores` rows that belong to a piece of content
(ad-hoc evaluations of pasted text carry the nil content id and are left out), `null` when there is none,
with the new `quality_score_count`. Both tiles (dashboard home and `/dashboard/analytics`) show
"Sem dados" for `null`, else `NN%` with "n avaliações". The published-ratio fallback and the unused
`ai_generation_metadata.quality_score` read are removed.

      `says there is no data without content scores`, pt-locale dashboard test, `says there is no data without a quality score`
