## Summary

Presentation-only polish of `/dashboard/personas`; no backend or persona data model change.

- Avatar: `personaInitial` (`modules/persona/components/persona-initial.ts`) takes the first Unicode letter
  (`\p{L}`, so "Á" counts; digits, punctuation, symbols and emoji are skipped) and uppercases it. A name without a
  letter shows the lucide `UserRound` icon with `role="img"` and "Avatar de {name}" / "Avatar of {name}".
- Fallbacks: `mapPersonaProfileToCardProps(profile, fallbacks)` takes the translated description and role from
  `PersonaTile` ("Sem descrição" / "No description", "Persona"); no English literal is left in the adapter.
- Card: Edit/Delete moved into the card footer (`footer` slot, `min-h-11 min-w-11`), cards share the row height,
  expertise chips are 12 px (were 10 px), areas beyond six show a "+N" chip with a screen-reader count, and the card no
  longer uses the hover variant (it is not clickable, so the pointer cursor and lift were misleading).
- Sliders: 44 px tall native range inputs with a cyan `focus-visible` ring, the value shown next to each (an
  `<output>`) and announced via `aria-valuetext`; on a phone the slider takes its own row under label and value.
- Empty state: "Nenhuma persona ainda" / "No personas yet" with a 44 px "Criar a primeira persona" button; a search
  without matches keeps "Nenhuma persona encontrada" and no button. Inline rgba literals replaced with `TEXT` /
  `TEXT_MUTED`.
- Phone: `page.tsx` applies `max-sm:` descendant classes so the search box and every form text box and button are at
  least 44 px tall below 640 px, without changing the shared atoms.
