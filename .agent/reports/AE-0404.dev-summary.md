# AE-0404 — Dev summary

Hotfix (T1, owner fast path). Branch `fix/ae-0404-schedule-picker-recommended`.

## What changed

The owner can now pick the day and then the time on Telegram, from slots ranked by the audience and our own posts. An auto-scheduled run can be moved.

- **Reaching the picker:** `🗓 Mudar dia/horário` is on every scheduled message, and `🗓 Agendar` is on the review keyboard.
- **The two steps:** step 1 shows 7 days with the best days starred. Step 2 shows the day's top 4 times with their reasons, plus `✏️ Outro horário` and `← Dias`.
- **Rescheduling:** a slot picked on a scheduled run moves it with a `scheduled → scheduled` compare-and-set.
- **Recommender:** a pure function that blends three signals, then picks the auto `next_slot` from the next 7 days:
  - Graph `online_followers`, cached daily in the new `instagram_audience_hours` table;
  - post engagement shrunk by `n/(n+5)`;
  - the plan prior (POV Tue/Thu 12:00, Radar 12/18/21).
- **With no data:** POV runs and Radar runs keep their old auto slot.

Decisions are recorded in the ticket's Decision Log.
