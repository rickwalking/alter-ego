### Summary

`CAROUSEL_AUTO_ARCHIVE_DAYS` (default 30, 0 = off) archive themselves on the workflow worker tick. An
archived carousel leaves the active workflow board, is listed under "Arquivados", and raises no stuck,
failed or failure-rate alert. `carousel_projects.archived_at` ships as an Alembic revision plus an
idempotent deploy one-shot, because prod does not run Alembic.
