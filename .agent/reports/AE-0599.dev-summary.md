## Root cause (evidence from read-only prod triage, counts and ids only)

First content gates after the AE-0588 deploy: `6f36130e` 31 violations,
`d8da820a` 39, `41c4f437` 1 (`translation_shape_mismatch`). The 12 gates
before the deploy had 0.

1. Bilingual draft blobs on most slides. Drafts whose `draft_text` is a PT+EN
   JSON object become `presentation_pt` + `presentation_en`, and the
   model-written EN wins over the EN translation (`_resolve_en_source`). Before
   the deploy 8 of 108 slides were blobs, all body-only. After it 12 of 18 POV
   slides were blobs, with feature lists and long headings. All 70 first-gate
   violations of `6f36130e` and `d8da820a` are on blob slides. The copy was 1.5
   to 2.7 times over budget: bodies 287 to 540 characters against 200, summary
   point bodies 149 to 322 against 90, headings 71 to 78 against 64. The likely
   trigger is the AE-0588 prompt pair: the limits block listed feature limits on
   every content slide and said the limits apply to "the English copy", and POV
   v3 dropped v1's role targets and only pointed at that block.
2. Blob EN uses the PT `**` markers. The bounded review repair converts them
   to `<strong>`, but it is all-or-nothing per payload. If a length blocker
   remains, or if the 13 extra characters of `<strong>` push a field over its
   budget, the conversion is thrown away and every emphasized EN field stays an
   `emphasis_markup_locale_mismatch`. This part is deterministic: with a
   cooperative fit model the pre-fix code still leaves 4 mismatches on the
   `d8da820a` shape.
3. In prod the shorten step landed no rewrite on any blob slide. The stored
   drafts, run through the real step with a no-op fit model, reproduce both
   first gates field for field (31 and 39). With a cooperative model the
   pre-fix code reaches 0 on `6f36130e` and 4 on `d8da820a` (cause 2). Why the
   prod rewrites were lost cannot be recovered: the logs went with the container
   restart before triage, and the Langfuse cloud traces were not in scope. The
   step now logs rejections by reason and voice reverts.

Why the earlier gates passed: blobs were rare and body-only. The bounded repair
trimmed the body at a sentence boundary and converted the EN emphasis in the
same pass. The new blobs carry items and long headings, which that repair cannot
shorten.

`41c4f437`: slide 4's EN translation kept a quote card (`insight`) that its PT
had lost. No length or markup path is involved. This is a separate follow-up.

## Fix (generation side only)

- `copy_budget_emphasis.py`: before measuring, every balanced emphasis pair is
  put in its locale's convention, using the same `normalize_emphasis` the review
  repair uses. Fit answers get the same conversion. The owner's hook heading is
  never touched.
- Logging: `rejected_reasons` in `copy_budget_fit_done`, and a new
  `copy_budget_fit_voice_reverted` event.
- Prompts:
  - `carousel_copy_budget/v2/slide_limits.yaml`: item limits are not a request
    to add items; states the markup cost per locale.
  - `carousel_pov/v4` and `carousel_pov_news/v4` `content.yaml`: this slide's
    contract limits are inlined before the answer format.
  - Defaults switched: `POV_PROMPT_CONTENT_VERSION = "v4"`,
    `COPY_BUDGET_LIMITS_PROMPT_VERSION = "v2"`. v1 and v3 are kept.
- Unchanged: gate, validators, limits, severities, contracts, voice threshold
  70, forbidden phrases, ADR-0030 checks, the gate repair and the bounded repair.
- No new LLM call. The fit call keeps its `copy budget fit` Langfuse trace.
