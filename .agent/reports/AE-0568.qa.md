## Findings

### External review (PR #303 at `20283244`): two blockers, all nits fixed

1. **Missing collapse control** — an expanded column (e.g. Published, 23
   cards) could never be collapsed again. Fixed: header collapse button,
   `aria-expanded` on both controls, expand → collapse persistence tested.
2. **Unsafe storage access** — unguarded `setItem`/`getItem` in
   board-scroll/board-sequence/the expanded-set write could throw inside the
   card link's onClick (blocking navigation) or on load, and a `null`
   `position.tops` crashed `applyBoardScroll`. Fixed: every access goes
   through the new `board-storage.ts` helper; payloads are validated through
   `unknown`; disabled storage, quota throws and corrupt/null payloads are
   tested, including a card click under a throwing storage.

Nits fixed: modifier keys (Cmd/Ctrl/Alt) no longer trigger the bracket
shortcuts; the prev/next controls are a labelled `role="group"`; the
breadcrumb nav label is i18n (`common.breadcrumbNavLabel`, PT/EN); the saved
scroll is keyed per board view (active vs archived) so Back restores the right
one; the `setTimeout(0)` hydration deferral is replaced by
`useSyncExternalStore` with a stable cached snapshot; the column headers keep
their place by construction (the list scrolls, not the header) and the
ticket/claims now say exactly that instead of "sticky".
