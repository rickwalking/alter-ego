## Developer Completion Report
Ticket: AE-0590 — Use a generated cover on slide 1
Status: In Development (hotfix T1; lead pushes and opens the PR)

### Summary
A cover generation that succeeds, the one the autopilot starts for a fresh carousel or the owner's "Gerar capa com
Grok" (Pedro, 2026-11-01), becomes slide 1's `slide_cover` (kind `ai_image`). The selection waits (bounded,
5 s polls, 45 min) until the workflow is paused at a gate. It then writes one conditional UPDATE of `slide_cover`
alone (`WHERE slide_cover IS NULL AND hook_asset_id IS NOT DISTINCT FROM <baseline>`) under the unchanged project lock
and lock-version CAS (`write_locked`). It never re-renders, never changes status and never publishes. The owner's
choice always wins: a cover already chosen at scheduling (the owner clicking Grok while a cover is chosen), or any
change since, is a logged skip. A carousel already completed or at final review keeps its cover, and the generated
one stays a picker option (no re-render, no re-approval). Failures change nothing; a CAS conflict or a held lock is
one logged skip, never retried. `PUT /hook-photo` now clears `slide_cover`, so the owner's latest photo wins. No
schema change.
