## What changed

- The persona judge's retry runs on the next model of the role's queue (`judge_voice(..., fallback)`,
  `carousel_slide_fix._fallback_judge` via `as_fallback()`).
- A fixed slide the judge could not score keeps its old score in `persona_scores`, marked
  `rescore_pending` / `unscored_ticks`. The next tick re-scores its current copy before any rewrite.
- The autopilot step waits (`persona_rescore_pending`, run stays `generating`) when only unscored
  slides hold the voice floor. After `AUTOPILOT_PERSONA_UNSCORED_MAX_TICKS` (default 3) unscored ticks
  it escalates `persona_judge_unavailable`. A real low score still escalates `persona_score_below_threshold`.
