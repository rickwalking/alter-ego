# AE-0608 — dev summary

## Change

Tests and tooling only. Nothing under `backend/src` changes. Root causes and decisions are in the ticket.

1. Event writes: tests wait for what the store answered through `SettledEventRecorder`. `carousel_consolidation`
   keeps the real 100 ms budget: its `client` gives the event recorder its own engine, so a cancelled write cannot
   reach the workflow's one `StaticPool` connection. That engine's connects are shielded and all closed at teardown.
2. Comment gate: reads comments through `ts.createSourceFile` and `iterateComments`, and skips files with no marker.
3. Langfuse and OpenTelemetry: `tests/conftest.py` forces blank keys and tracing off before anything is imported.
4. Clocks and order: `revive_stale` gets an injected clock, the archive test dates its row at test time, and all three
   sweep-count files use their own database through `sweep_engine`.

No limit, gate, skip, retry, filter or production value changed.
